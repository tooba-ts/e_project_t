What Guides Us

Mission & Vision

Keeping Time, Earning Trust

Our mission is simple: to keep every timepiece - from a cherished heirloom to a brand-new smartwatch - running exactly as it should, and to make the people who own them feel looked after.

Our vision is to remain the most trusted full-service watch house in America, where repair, appraisal and retail live under one roof and every customer is treated like a member of the family.

  • Honest advice before every sale, repair or appraisal
  • Genuine parts and factory-grade tooling, always
  • Fair, transparent pricing with no hidden surprises
  • A warm welcome for first-timers and collectors alike
